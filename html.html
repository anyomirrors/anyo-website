The fine print

How the ₱100 reward works

  • ₱100 is earned for each qualified, successful referral.
  • The referred customer must be a new ANYO customer.
  • The referral code must be given before or during the order.
  • The order must be successfully paid and completed.
  • Cancelled, refunded, or fraudulent orders do not qualify.
  • Self-referrals are not allowed.
  • ANYO may verify referrals before releasing the reward.
  • Rewards follow ANYO's current campaign period and rules.
  • ANYO may reject invalid or fraudulent referrals.
